- No swaps were needed: all 10 candidates from the brief are directly cited in the researched docs, are absent from the already-built list, and have a laptop-runnable data path in `data/`.
- Ranking uses existing `bm25/`, `embeddings.npy`, `faiss.index` rather than new indices; A/B and shadow items replay `online_sessions.jsonl`/`commerce.db` rather than live traffic.
